# M8-D — Integrated magnetic workflow closure

M8-D closes the magnetic workflow by comparing only already verified M6
electronic states.  It adds no calculation, executor, capability, scheduler,
launcher, retry/recovery channel, or generic runtime authority.

## Boundary

The accepted dataflow is:

```text
magnetic candidate intent
  -> existing M6 final-SCF
  -> qraft.electronic-state + qraft.magnetic-state
  -> engine-owned final-energy evidence
  -> M8-D deterministic comparison
  -> qraft.magnetic-selection / exact selected-state reference
```

M8-A, M8-B, and M8-C remain the authorities for collinear, non-collinear,
and full-SOC intent, rendering, output parsing, and magnetic observation.
M8-D never infers spin physics from an energy.  It consumes the common,
verified `qraft.magnetic-state` evidence published by those milestones.

## Final-energy evidence

New M6 electronic states may contain an optional `final_scf.final_energy`.
Its SIESTA engine parser accepts only the native final `siesta: Total = ...`
inside the post-convergence `siesta: Final energy (eV):` section following
`Using DM_out to compute the final energy and forces`.  Intermediate SCF
energies are intentionally not selection evidence.

The evidence binds quantity, finite eV value, parser version, converged-SCF
claim, final-FDF SHA-256, and stdout SHA-256.  Missing, ambiguous, truncated,
non-converged, non-finite, or hash-mismatched output is not selectable.
Historic M6/M7 electronic-state artifacts remain readable.  They are
ineligible for automatic M8-D selection until a separate immutable
`qraft.final-scf-energy` envelope, generated by the SIESTA engine parser,
binds its evidence to the exact electronic-state, magnetic-state, identity,
FDF, and stdout hashes.  A caller-supplied numeric value is never accepted.

## Comparability and policy

The selection protocol verifies each electronic state through the existing M7
handoff verifier before comparison.  Each candidate binds candidate id, spin
mode, electronic-state file/content hashes, magnetic-state file/content hashes,
scientific identity, final energy, and the energy-source hashes.

Automatic ranking requires identical effective final-SCF comparison context:

- composition/species mapping and final geometry semantics;
- XC evidence and pseudopotential SHA-256 values;
- the relevant final numerical SCF FDF settings;
- the same `siesta.final_total_energy` definition and eV unit.

M8-D records energy per atom and delta from the minimum.  A caller must supply
`energy_tolerance_ev_per_atom`.  Candidates tied at or below that tolerance
produce `REVIEW_REQUIRED`/`DEGENERATE`; there is no alphabetical, insertion,
or host-dependent tie-break.  Any required missing, corrupt, or incompatible
candidate produces `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, never a partial automatic selection.
Scalar collinear states are not ranked together with non-collinear or SOC
states unless they independently satisfy this full comparison context.

The result is an immutable `qraft.magnetic-selection` scientific artifact.
Its selected reference includes exact electronic-state and magnetic-state hashes
and its canonical selection hash excludes paths, scheduler placement, MPI,
host, and timestamps.

## Real M8-A evidence reuse

No new SIESTA calculation is needed to establish the integration behavior.
The accepted M8-A fcc Fe FM/AFM M6 artifacts were re-read and their native
final-SCF stdout was parsed by the M8-D engine parser:

- FM: `-6890.936315 eV` (`-3445.4681575 eV/atom`);
- AFM: `-6890.816388 eV` (`-3445.408194 eV/atom`);
- delta AFM–FM: `+0.119927 eV` (`+0.059963499999867054 eV/atom`).

With explicit `0.001 eV/atom` tolerance, FM is the sole selected candidate.
The derived artifact has file SHA-256
`a037f09de0dd182d8e22b38591b19d70977f81f810cbfa77cca9976171413362` and
content SHA-256
`f728e63a9994e4f6de7006fd90969ae616a1f2d17c18c2905aeb51e420afc317`.
This is evidence reuse only: `NEW_SIESTA_EXECUTIONS=0`.

## Compatibility

Existing M6/M7 state verification is preserved.  Collinear FM/AFM identities
remain distinct; M8-B direction-sensitive non-collinear identities and M8-C
direction-sensitive SOC identities remain distinct; changing resources does
not change scientific identity.  The historic non-magnetic identity
`8e8723a8216fd0f0f6dfb0cbf61ee1da3f7381162878b85431255ef380785522` remains
unchanged.

The authoritative native regression recorded `658 passed`, `1 skipped`, zero
failures, and exit code zero on Python 3.13.14 / pytest 9.0.3.  M8-D therefore
closes M8.  M9 remains `NOT_STARTED`.
