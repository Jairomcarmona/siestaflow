# Community conduct

QRAFT welcomes respectful participation in issues, pull requests, reviews and
project discussions. Critique ideas and code constructively, acknowledge
contributions, and make room for people with different experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks and disclosure
of another person's private information are unacceptable. Keep disagreements
focused on the technical or scientific question and respect requests to stop
unwelcome interaction.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, restrict participation, or
close discussions to enforce these expectations. They should explain moderation
decisions when doing so will not expose private information.

For a public moderation request, mention a maintainer in the affected discussion
without including sensitive details. For a confidential concern, first arrange
a private contact channel with a maintainer; do not post personal information
or private evidence in a public issue. The current maintainer is listed in
`pyproject.toml`.
